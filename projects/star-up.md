    <h1>STAR-UP</h1>
    <p class="case-study__summary">
      An AI platform designed to connect researchers, founders, and investors by turning fragmented discovery and matching into a clearer product workflow.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="case-study__meta">
    <span>Role: AI platform builder</span>
    <span>Context: BAD1 Hackathon</span>
    <span>Result: 1st place overall and Track 1 winner</span>
  </div>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Problem</h2>
    <p>
      Early-stage ecosystems have a discovery problem: researchers, founders, and investors often have complementary interests, but the matching process is scattered across networks, documents, and ad hoc conversations.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What We Built</h2>
    <p>
      STAR-UP was framed as an AI-assisted matching platform rather than only a search feature. The idea was to help different participants surface relevant people, opportunities, and alignment signals with less manual filtering.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Focused the product around discovery and matching workflows instead of generic AI summarization.</li>
      <li>Used the hackathon format to turn a platform idea into a concrete public proof point.</li>
      <li>Built the project strongly enough to win the overall prize and Track 1.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Why It Mattered</h2>
    <p>
      This project is useful in the portfolio because it shows more than model usage. It shows product framing, marketplace thinking, and the ability to package an AI idea into something legible to judges and stakeholders.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>AI was used in service of platform value, not as decoration.</li>
      <li>The concept was broad enough to feel ambitious, but narrow enough to demo.</li>
      <li>The outcome created a strong public signal through both the award and the mayor presentation invitation.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What This Demonstrates</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Ability to frame AI work as a platform product with clear participants and value flows.</li>
      <li>Comfort moving from concept to demo under tight time constraints.</li>
      <li>Strong communication of product direction, not only implementation detail.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
