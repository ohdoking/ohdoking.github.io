    <p class="case-study__summary">
      A renewable energy portfolio backend service where the work focused on core backend implementation and improving ingestion connector reliability.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="case-study__meta">
    <span>Role: Backend developer</span>
    <span>Context: Ampero GmbH</span>
    <span>Focus: Kotlin backend services, ingestion pipelines, reliability</span>
  </div>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Problem</h2>
    <p>
      Energy portfolio software depends on stable ingestion and trustworthy domain data. When connectors are slow, brittle, or hard to reason about, the product suffers long before users see an obvious failure screen.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What I Worked On</h2>
    <p>
      I contributed to version 2 of the service and focused on backend implementation work that improved the flow and reliability of data ingestion. The challenge was less about one feature and more about the dependability of domain data moving through the system.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Worked with Kotlin, Spring Boot, Hibernate, SAP HANA, AWS, and Kubernetes.</li>
      <li>Improved ingestion connector pipelines used by the renewable energy portfolio service.</li>
      <li>Helped make backend behavior more reliable for downstream product use.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>
      This project is a strong portfolio entry because it reflects the less glamorous but highly valuable side of backend work: making domain data pipelines more reliable so the product can stay trustworthy.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Data pipeline quality directly affects product confidence.</li>
      <li>Backend improvements matter even when they are not user-facing features.</li>
      <li>Operational discipline is especially important in domain-heavy systems.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What This Demonstrates</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Experience improving production backend systems in a domain-specific environment.</li>
      <li>Ability to work on reliability problems that sit between application logic and data movement.</li>
      <li>Comfort with backend delivery in cloud-hosted business systems.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
