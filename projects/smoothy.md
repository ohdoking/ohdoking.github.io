    <h1>Smoothy</h1>
    <p class="case-study__summary">
      A dual-brain AI voice companion for elderly users, built to combine natural real-time conversation with a second path for health-related monitoring and assistance.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="case-study__meta">
    <span>Role: Builder and backend/AI contributor</span>
    <span>Context: Big Berlin Hack 2026</span>
    <span>Result: 1st place overall, telli and ai-coustics Voice AI Track winner</span>
  </div>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Problem</h2>
    <p>
      Many AI voice demos sound polished for a few turns but do not feel trustworthy when the interaction matters. For elderly support scenarios, responsiveness, clarity, and product framing matter more than showing a generic chatbot with a microphone.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What We Built</h2>
    <p>
      Smoothy approached the problem as a voice product rather than only an LLM wrapper. The idea was to let one path optimize for immediate conversational flow while another path handled more deliberate analysis and monitoring.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Focused on natural interaction quality instead of broad feature lists.</li>
      <li>Combined real-time voice behavior with a second AI path for additional support logic.</li>
      <li>Packaged the project as a clear, human-centered product story rather than a model showcase.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Why It Worked</h2>
    <p>
      The project became credible because the architecture served a specific user interaction problem. It was easier for judges to understand the product value, the user context, and the role of the technical design.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Strong problem framing around elderly users.</li>
      <li>Clear distinction between interaction quality and deeper AI assistance.</li>
      <li>A presentation shape that made the system legible as a product, not just a demo.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What This Demonstrates</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Ability to shape AI work into a convincing user-facing product narrative.</li>
      <li>Experience building under hackathon constraints without losing technical clarity.</li>
      <li>Practical thinking about latency, interaction design, and applied AI systems.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
