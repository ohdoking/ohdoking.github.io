    <h1>AI Safety Chrome Extension</h1>
    <p class="case-study__summary">
      A local AI browser extension designed to protect kids from toxic AI content while keeping the interaction lightweight and privacy-aware.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="case-study__meta">
    <span>Role: AI and browser tooling builder</span>
    <span>Context: SAP and KPMG AI Cybersecurity Hackathon 2025</span>
    <span>Result: 1st place overall</span>
  </div>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Problem</h2>
    <p>
      AI safety often gets discussed at a policy level, but parents and users face product-level exposure problems directly in the browser. The challenge was to make a safety tool that is practical, understandable, and fast enough to feel useful in real usage.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What We Built</h2>
    <p>
      The project focused on a local AI browser extension that could detect or flag toxic AI content while reducing the need to send everything to a remote service. That constraint made privacy and product ergonomics part of the architecture.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Used local AI capabilities to keep the tool lightweight and privacy-aware.</li>
      <li>Framed the solution around a concrete safety use case instead of abstract cybersecurity language.</li>
      <li>Turned a browser-side constraint into part of the product differentiation.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>Why It Worked</h2>
    <p>
      The strength of the project was its clarity. The problem, the intervention point, and the user value were all easy to explain, which made the technical choices easier to trust.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Strong user framing around child safety.</li>
      <li>Good fit between local AI constraints and privacy expectations.</li>
      <li>A focused scope that was easier to evaluate than a broad “AI safety platform.”</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="case-study__section">
    <h2>What This Demonstrates</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Ability to productize AI in a user-facing environment with tight platform constraints.</li>
      <li>Practical thinking about privacy, speed, and interface boundaries.</li>
      <li>Comfort building small but credible AI tools with a clear outcome.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
